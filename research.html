Research & Publications

Institutional research and field contributions

Produced as a researcher at Visionary Analytics, Vilnius, and through fieldwork in African development contexts. Work spans quantitative modelling, econometric analysis, stakeholder interviews, and policy writing.

Named publications
EESC 2025 Co-author

Socially Just Transition in the Green Deal: Cost of Inaction

Assesses how a prospective EU Just Transition Directive could shape the social and employment outcomes of the European Green Deal. Draws on literature review, stakeholder interviews, foresight-based PESTEL scenario building, and partial-equilibrium projections across seven Member States representing diverse welfare and production regimes.

Contributions
  • Scenario analysis and econometric modelling of macroeconomic outcomes to 2045
  • Partial-equilibrium modelling comparing BAU, weak, and strong JTD scenarios
  • Quantitative analysis of employment, poverty, skills shortages, and regional disparities across 7 Member States
  • Visualised macroeconomic impact projections for policymaker audiences
  • Contributed to written analysis of regional spillover effects and policy recommendations

Bridges green transition policy, labour market governance, and evidence-based social policy at continental scale. Published December 2025.
